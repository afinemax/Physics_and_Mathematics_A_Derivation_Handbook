Starting from Maxwell's equations in vacuum,
\begin{align}
\nabla\cdot\mathbf E &= 0, & \nabla\cdot\mathbf B &= 0,\\
\nabla\times\mathbf E &= -\pdv{\mathbf B}{t}, &
\nabla\times\mathbf B &= \mu_0\epsilon_0\pdv{\mathbf E}{t}.
\end{align}
Taking the curl of Faraday's law and using
$\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf E)=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf E)-\nabla^2\mathbf E$ gives
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\nabla^2\mathbf E-\mu_0\epsilon_0\pdv[2]{\mathbf E}{t}=0.}
\end{equation}
Thus the wave speed is $c=1/\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}$.
